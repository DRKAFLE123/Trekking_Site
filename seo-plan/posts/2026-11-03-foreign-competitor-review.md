## Changes needed before publishing

**92 EBC December**
- Flights: one source says Ramechhap until about 30 Nov, then Kathmandu. Needs confirmation from our ticketing contact.
- Buffer: first-person reports suggest 3–4 spare days in winter; we say 1–2. Check with Krishna.
- Add first-hand detail competitors win with (in-moments): batteries die in minutes on Kala Patthar, toilets freeze overnight, sunburn. Microspikes after about 20 Dec.
- Temperature table agrees with trekroute's published means (Gorak Shep min −17.8°C), so ours holds.

**93 Poon Hill**: proposed title "Poon Hill Trek Itinerary: 3, 4 & 5 Days, Distance & Difficulty" (58).
- Add per-day km and height gain (bookatrekking: 40.3 km 5-day, 49.3 km 7-day; +2,370 m), a route map, Poon Hill gate fee (NPR 100), food prices.
- Our "30–40 km" should become about 40 km.
- Price problem: our page is USD 1,066; bookatrekking €630–730 (5–7 days), a Western blog paid about USD 240 from Pokhara. A foreign reader who compares will not enquire.

**94 ABC**: proposed title "Annapurna Base Camp Trek in December & Winter: Is It Safe?" (58). Add sections answering "is it open in December" and "is it dangerous" as direct questions.

**95 Best Treks**: my Langtang section is out of date. TAAN (22 Oct) and Onlinekhabar (29 Oct) report Langtang reopened via Dhunche and Thulo Syabru after the August flood; no hotels in Syabrubesi as of late Oct. Our itinerary already drives to Thulo Syabru. Add a dated status table with sources: Langtang, Manaslu (open "with extreme caution", NTNC notice 21 Oct), Annapurna Circuit road repaired, Thorong La snow in autumn.

## Why there are no enquiries yet (assessment)

1. Almost all our new content is research-stage. Buyer-stage searches appear in autocomplete and we target none: everest base camp trek uk companies, cost uk, packages from uk, nepal trekking companies, nepal walking holidays, nepal hiking holidays, private trekking guide nepal.
2. Biggest question across every market that we don't answer: can you trek in nepal without a guide / nepal trekking without guide 2026. Also best treks in nepal for first time trekkers, easiest trek in nepal, cheapest trek in nepal, is it safe to trek to everest base camp.
3. Trust: bookatrekking shows Trustpilot, free cancellation up to 8 weeks, 25% deposit, currency selector. Our UK phone and reps exist but aren't sold.
4. Price: EBC USD 1,566 beats Exodus (about EUR 2,480) and G Adventures (USD 1,679): a selling point to use. Poon Hill is the opposite.
5. Authority: trek pages sit at 40–80. Content alone won't move them; reviews and links are required.
