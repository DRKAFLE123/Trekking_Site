# Everest Helicopter Tour Cost 2026: Shared vs Private Prices

**Quick answer:** An Everest helicopter tour from Kathmandu costs about **US$1,000 to US$1,700 per person** on a shared flight and **US$4,500 to US$7,500** to charter the whole helicopter, which carries up to five passengers on the lower legs. The tour takes four to five hours, usually includes a short landing at Kala Patthar (5,545m) facing Everest and breakfast at Hotel Everest View. ## What happens on the tour

Most tours follow the same route:

1. **Kathmandu to Lukla** (about 45 minutes), with a refuelling stop.
2. **Lukla to Kala Patthar**, flying up the Dudh Koshi valley past Namche Bazaar and Tengboche.
3. **Landing at Kala Patthar** (5,545m), the classic viewpoint for Everest, for a few minutes of photos. Some tours fly over Everest Base Camp and the Khumbu Icefall instead of landing at Base Camp itself.
4. **Breakfast at Hotel Everest View** in Syangboche (about 3,880m), with Everest, Lhotse and Ama Dablam in front of you.
5. **Back to Kathmandu** via Lukla, usually before lunch.

At this altitude a helicopter can only lift two or three passengers at a time, so on the highest leg the pilot often shuttles passengers in turns from a lower point such as Lukla or Pheriche. Landing time is kept short on purpose: you go from about 1,400m to 5,545m in under an hour, and the risk of altitude sickness rises with every minute up there.

## What is included

On a good tour, the price should include:

- Hotel pick-up and drop-off in Kathmandu
- The helicopter flight from Kathmandu and back, with refuelling
- The Sagarmatha National Park entry fee
- Breakfast at Hotel Everest View
- Taxes

Our tour includes all of these, plus lunch in Thamel after you land. It does not include travel insurance, tips for the crew, or extra nights in Kathmandu if weather delays the flight.

## What changes the price

- **Shared or private:** a private charter costs four to five times a shared seat, but split between five people it can cost about the same per person.
- **Season:** autumn (October and November) and spring (March to May) are busiest; prices and availability follow demand.
- **Landing:** tours that land at Kala Patthar cost more than scenic flights that only fly past the mountains.
- **Taxes and fees:** check whether VAT and the national park fee are included.
- **Weight:** passengers and luggage are weighed; heavy groups may need an extra rotation.

## Is the Everest helicopter tour worth it?

It is the only way to see Everest up close in a single morning, without two weeks of trekking or any risk of altitude sickness building up over days. It suits travellers short on time, older travellers, families and anyone who cannot trek. The trade-off is cost and time on the ground: you spend minutes, not days, among the mountains.

## Safety, weather and altitude

- **Weather:** flights only go in clear weather, usually early in the morning. Keep a spare day in Kathmandu in case your flight is postponed.
- **Altitude:** a few minutes at 5,545m is usually fine for healthy people, but tell the operator about heart or lung conditions, pregnancy or recent illness. If you feel dizzy, return to the helicopter.
- **Cold:** it is below freezing at Kala Patthar even on sunny days; bring a warm jacket, hat and gloves.
- **Insurance:** make sure your [travel insurance](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/travel-insurance-for-trekking-in-nepal) covers helicopter flights and high altitude.

## Best time for an Everest helicopter tour

October to December and March to May give the clearest mornings. Winter (December to February) is cold but often very clear. The June to September monsoon brings cloud and frequent cancellations.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an Everest helicopter tour cost per person?

About US$1,000 to US$1,700 per person on a shared flight. A private charter costs about US$4,500 to US$7,500 for up to five passengers.

### Does the helicopter land at Everest Base Camp?

Usually not at Base Camp itself. Most tours land at Kala Patthar (5,545m), which has a better view of Everest, and fly over Base Camp and the Khumbu Icefall.

### How long do you spend at Kala Patthar?

A few minutes, because of the altitude. You spend longer at breakfast at Hotel Everest View, about 3,880m.

### How long does the Everest helicopter tour take?

Four to five hours from Kathmandu and back, including the refuelling stop and breakfast.

### Can you fly over the top of Everest by helicopter?

No. Tours fly below the summit. Only one pilot has ever landed a helicopter on the summit; read [can a helicopter fly to the top of Mount Everest](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/can-a-helicopter-fly-on-top-of-mount-everest).

### Is the Everest helicopter tour safe?

Operators fly only in good weather with experienced mountain pilots. The main risk for passengers is altitude, which is why landings at Kala Patthar are kept short.
