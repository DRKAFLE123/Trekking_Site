# Manaslu Circuit Trek Cost 2026: Permits, Prices & Budget

**Quick answer:** A 14 to 16 day Manaslu Circuit trek usually costs **US$1,100 to US$1,800 per person** with a registered agency, which you must use because Manaslu is a restricted area. Permits alone come to about **US$150 to US$180** in autumn. The rest is transport, teahouse rooms and food, and your guide and porter. Our 16-day private [Manaslu Circuit trek](https://natureheaventreks.com/trips/manaslu-circuit-trek) is priced from US$1,466 per person.

## Manaslu Circuit cost at a glance

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Permits (RAP, MCAP, ACAP) | about US$150–180 in autumn, US$115–145 off-season |
| Transport Kathmandu to Machha Khola and back | US$30–60 per person in a shared private jeep |
| Teahouse rooms | US$5–8 a night for a basic room |
| Food and drinks | US$20–30 a day, more above Samagaon |
| Licensed guide | US$25–35 a day |
| Porter | US$18–25 a day |
| Total with an agency, 14–16 days | about US$1,100–1,800 per person |

Prices are typical 2026 rates from operators and teahouses; your total depends on group size, season and comfort.

## Manaslu permit fees in 2026

You need three permits. The Restricted Area Permit (RAP) can only be issued through a registered trekking agency, and you must trek with a licensed guide.

| Permit | September to November | December to August |
|---|---|---|
| Manaslu Restricted Area Permit (RAP) | US$100 for the first 7 days, then US$15 a day | US$75 for the first 7 days, then US$10 a day |
| Manaslu Conservation Area Permit (MCAP) | NPR 3,000 (about US$22) | NPR 3,000 |
| Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) | NPR 3,000 (about US$22) | NPR 3,000 |

You need the ACAP because the trail ends on the Annapurna side, past Bimthang.

**What this means for a real trip:** on a standard 16-day itinerary like ours you spend about nine days inside the restricted area. That makes the RAP US$130 in autumn (US$100 plus two extra days at US$15) or US$95 off-season, so all three permits together come to about US$175 in autumn and about US$140 from December to August.

If you add the Tsum Valley, it has its own restricted area permit; see our [Manaslu and Tsum Valley trek](https://natureheaventreks.com/trips/manaslu-tsum-valley-trek). Fees are set by the government and can change, so we confirm them when you book. See our [trekking permits page](https://natureheaventreks.com/travel-info/trekking-permits-and-fees).

## Manaslu trek cost for Indian and Nepali trekkers

- **Indian and other SAARC nationals** pay NPR 1,000 each for the MCAP and ACAP instead of NPR 3,000. Agencies report the restricted area permit differently for SAARC nationals: some charge the standard rate and some a lower one. Ask for the exact figure when you get a quote. The rule that you trek through an agency with a licensed guide applies to you too.
- **Nepali citizens** pay NPR 100 each for the MCAP and ACAP, and are reported not to need the restricted area permit.

## Transport costs

| Route | Public bus | Shared jeep (per seat) | Private jeep (per vehicle) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kathmandu to Soti Khola or Machha Khola | NPR 1,200–2,000 | NPR 2,500–4,000 | US$160–260 |
| Dharapani or Besisahar to Kathmandu | NPR 1,000–1,500 | NPR 2,000–3,500 | US$120–180 |
| Besisahar to Pokhara | NPR 800–1,200 | — | US$100–150 |

The drive to Machha Khola takes 8 to 9 hours on a rough road. A private jeep shared by four to six people works out at about US$30–60 each and is the most comfortable option. After the August 2026 floods, some drivers used a longer route via Benighat and Arughat, so check the road when you book.

## Teahouse rooms and food

Rooms are simple: usually a bed and blanket for US$5–8 a night, with a few lodges offering an attached bathroom for US$15–20. Hot showers, Wi-Fi and charging cost extra, usually US$2–5 each.

Dal bhat (rice, lentils and vegetables, with free refills) costs about US$5–7 lower down and more in Samagaon and Samdo, where everything arrives by mule or porter. Noodles, fried rice and soup are US$4–8, and tea or coffee US$2–4. Plan on US$20–30 a day for food, or US$25–40 a day for food and a room together.

## Guide and porter costs

A licensed guide is required on the Manaslu Circuit. Expect about US$25–35 a day, which covers the guide's food, lodging and insurance. A porter costs about US$18–25 a day and carries up to 20–25kg, usually shared between two trekkers. Through an agency, both are insured and paid fairly, and you are not left arranging replacements on the trail.

## Other costs to budget for

- **Travel insurance** that covers trekking to 5,500m and helicopter evacuation. The Larke Pass is 5,106m. See our [travel insurance guide](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/travel-insurance-for-trekking-in-nepal).
- **Gear** you rent or buy in Kathmandu, such as a down jacket or sleeping bag.
- **Tips** for your guide and porter at the end of the trek.
- **A reserve** for delays, extra nights or an unplanned jeep.

## Total budget by trek style

| Style | Per person | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | US$900–1,200 | Basic lodges, local transport, guide shared by a group |
| Mid-range | US$1,300–1,600 | Private jeep, better rooms where available, guide and porter |
| Premium | US$2,000+ | Private trip, best available lodges, extra days and services |

Group departures cost less per person because the guide and jeep are shared; private treks cost more but run on your dates and pace.

## Is the Manaslu Circuit open in 2026?

The trail was damaged by floods along the Budhi Gandaki in September and October 2026. TAAN's advisory of 23 October said the Manaslu Circuit to Larke Pass had reopened after partial repairs, starting from Machha Khola via Jagat with a short bypass near Yaru Bagar, and advised trekkers to take care. The Tsum Valley was still closed on that date after a bridge collapse. We check the latest status for your dates when you book; see also [is Nepal safe to visit after the 2026 floods](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/is-it-safe-to-travel-to-nepal-after-floods-2026).

## How to keep the cost down

- Share a private jeep with other trekkers.
- Bring snacks from Kathmandu; prices rise with altitude.
- Carry a refillable bottle and purification tablets or a filter instead of buying bottled water.
- Go from December to August if you can trek in colder or wetter weather: the restricted area permit is cheaper. Read our guide to [crossing the Larke Pass](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/does-the-manaslu-circuit-trek-need-to-cross-the-larkya-la-pass-at-5106-m) before choosing winter.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does the Manaslu Circuit trek cost?

Usually US$1,100 to US$1,800 per person for 14 to 16 days with a registered agency, including permits, transport, lodges, food and a guide.

### How much are Manaslu trek permits in 2026?

The restricted area permit is US$100 for the first 7 days plus US$15 a day from September to November, or US$75 plus US$10 a day from December to August. The MCAP and ACAP are NPR 3,000 each.

### Can I do the Manaslu Circuit without a guide?

No. Manaslu is a restricted area, and the permit is only issued through a registered agency for trekkers with a licensed guide.

### How much does the Manaslu trek cost for Indians?

Indian trekkers pay NPR 1,000 each for the MCAP and ACAP. Agencies report the restricted area permit for SAARC nationals differently, so ask for the exact figure in your quote.

### Is the Manaslu Circuit cheaper than the Annapurna Circuit?

No. It usually costs more because of the restricted area permit and the required guide. Compare the two in our [Manaslu vs Annapurna Circuit guide](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/manaslu-circuit-trek-vs-annapurna-circuit-trek).

### How many days do you need for the Manaslu Circuit?

Most itineraries take 14 to 16 days from Kathmandu, including two acclimatisation days at Samagaon and Samdo.
