# Everest Base Camp Trek Cost 2026: Full Price Breakdown

**Quick answer:** The Everest Base Camp trek costs about **US$1,100 to US$1,400** if you go independently and **US$1,300 to US$3,000** as a guided package of 12 to 16 days, depending on what is included. The biggest single costs are the return flight to Lukla (about US$450 to US$500) and food and lodging on the trail (US$35 to US$60 a day). Our 14-day [Everest Base Camp trek](https://natureheaventreks.com/trips/everest-base-camp-trek) is priced from US$1,566 per person and includes the Lukla flights, all meals on the trek, a licensed guide, a porter and permits.

## EBC trek cost at a glance

| Item | Typical cost per person |
|---|---|
| Sagarmatha National Park permit | NPR 3,000 (about US$22) |
| Khumbu Pasang Lhamu trek card | NPR 3,000 (about US$22) for most foreigners |
| Return flight Kathmandu–Lukla | about US$450–500 |
| Teahouse rooms | US$5–15 a night, more at Gorak Shep |
| Food and drinks | US$25–45 a day, rising with altitude |
| Licensed guide | US$25–35 a day |
| Porter | US$18–25 a day, often shared by two |
| Independent total, 12–14 days | about US$1,100–1,400 |
| Guided package, 12–16 days | about US$1,300–3,000 |

Figures are typical 2026 rates from lodges, airlines and operators; check current prices when you book.

## Permits and fees

You need two permits for Everest Base Camp:

| Permit | Most foreigners | SAARC nationals | Nepali citizens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sagarmatha National Park | NPR 3,000 | NPR 1,500 | NPR 100 |
| Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality trek card | NPR 3,000 | NPR 2,000 | not charged |

That is about NPR 6,000 (about US$45) for most foreign trekkers. The local trek card replaces the TIMS card in the Everest region. Some counters add VAT on top of the park fee, and published figures for the trek card vary between NPR 2,000 and 3,000, so carry a little extra. Our [trekking permits page](https://natureheaventreks.com/travel-info/trekking-permits-and-fees) has the details.

## Lukla flights

Almost everyone flies to Lukla, the start of the trek. A one-way ticket for foreigners costs about US$220 to US$250, so plan on US$450 to US$500 return. In busy seasons, many flights leave from Ramechhap (Manthali), a 4 to 5 hour drive from Kathmandu, instead of Kathmandu itself; in winter, most go from Kathmandu.

Lukla flights are often delayed by weather. Keep at least one spare day at the end of your trip, two in winter. Read more in our [EBC in December guide](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/everest-base-camp-trek-in-december).

## Teahouse food and rooms

Rooms in the Khumbu are simple and cheap: about US$5 to US$15 a night lower down, and US$10 to US$30 at Lobuche and Gorak Shep, where some lodges also expect you to eat dinner there. Namche Bazaar and Lukla have lodges with attached bathrooms.

Food is the bigger cost, and it rises with every day you climb. Dal bhat (rice, lentils and vegetables, with free refills) costs about US$6 lower down and US$10 to US$15 at Gorak Shep. Plan on:

- **US$25 to US$35 a day** for food below Namche
- **US$35 to US$45 a day** higher up
- **US$3 to US$5** each for a hot shower, charging or Wi-Fi

Bottled water is expensive high up; bring a filter or purification tablets and refill instead.

## Guide and porter costs

The Everest region's local government does not require a guide, unlike most of Nepal (see [can you trek in Nepal without a guide](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/trekking-in-nepal-without-a-guide)). Most first-time trekkers still take one. A licensed guide costs about US$25 to US$35 a day, including the guide's food and lodging, and a porter about US$18 to US$25 a day, usually shared between two trekkers. On a 12-day trek, a guide adds about US$300 to US$420 and a shared porter about US$110 to US$150 per person.

## Independent vs guided: total cost

| | Independent | Guided package |
|---|---|---|
| Permits | about US$45 | included |
| Lukla flights | about US$450–500 | usually included |
| Rooms and food, 12 days | about US$450–650 | usually included |
| Guide and porter | none | included |
| Extras (showers, charging, snacks) | US$100–150 | US$100–150 |

Packages vary widely. International operators list 14 to 16 day EBC trips from about US$1,300 to over US$2,900 per person, and some do not include meals. When you compare prices, check whether the quote includes:

- Lukla flights, and the transfer to Ramechhap if needed
- Breakfast, lunch and dinner on the trek
- A licensed guide and a porter
- Both permits
- A sleeping bag and down jacket

Our 14-day trek includes all of these from US$1,566. It does not include hotels in Kathmandu, tips, snacks, showers or charging.

## Other costs to budget for

- **Travel insurance** covering trekking to 6,000m and helicopter evacuation. A rescue from the Khumbu can cost several thousand dollars. See our [travel insurance guide](https://natureheaventreks.com/blogs/travel-insurance-for-trekking-in-nepal).
- **Nepal visa**: on arrival at Kathmandu airport.
- **Kathmandu hotels and meals** before and after the trek.
- **Tips** for your guide and porter at the end.
- **Gear** you buy or rent in Kathmandu.

## EBC trek cost for Indian and Nepali trekkers

- **Indian and other SAARC nationals** pay NPR 1,500 for the Sagarmatha National Park permit and about NPR 2,000 for the local trek card, so about NPR 3,500 in total. Ask your airline or agency about Lukla fares for your nationality.
- **Nepali citizens** pay NPR 100 for the park and are not charged the local trek card.

## How to save money on the EBC trek

- Trek in a small group to share the guide and porter.
- Fly from Ramechhap when that is the departure point; the fare is lower, though you add a long drive.
- Eat dal bhat (free refills) and bring snacks from Kathmandu.
- Use a filter instead of buying bottled water.
- If you are short on time rather than money, the 7-day [Everest View trek](https://natureheaventreks.com/trips/everest-view-trek) costs less, or see Everest in a morning on an [EBC helicopter tour](https://natureheaventreks.com/trips/everest-base-camp-heli-tour).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does the Everest Base Camp trek cost?

About US$1,100 to US$1,400 independently, or US$1,300 to US$3,000 for a 12 to 16 day guided package, depending on what is included.

### How much are Everest Base Camp permits in 2026?

About NPR 6,000 for most foreigners: NPR 3,000 for the Sagarmatha National Park permit and about NPR 3,000 for the Khumbu Pasang Lhamu trek card.

### How much is a flight to Lukla?

About US$220 to US$250 each way for foreigners, so US$450 to US$500 return.

### How much money should I carry on the EBC trek?

Carry cash in Nepali rupees: about US$35 to US$60 a day if you are paying for your own food and lodging, or US$10 to US$15 a day on a full-board package for showers, charging, snacks and drinks. ATMs exist in Lukla and Namche but can be unreliable.

### Is the Everest Base Camp trek expensive?

It costs more than most Nepal treks because of the Lukla flights. A full guided package with flights, meals, a guide and a porter typically costs US$1,300 to US$2,000 for two weeks.

### How much does the EBC trek cost for Indians?

The trek costs the same apart from permits: about NPR 3,500 in total for SAARC nationals instead of about NPR 6,000.
