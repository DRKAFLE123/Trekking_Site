1. Summary

The website redesign and the move from the old domain were completed earlier in October. This week the focus moved to search traffic: understanding what Google already shows, fixing everything that was holding the pages back, and starting the regular content programme (one new article every 2–3 days).

  • Google is showing the site far more often. Since 14 October, when Google processed the domain move, the site appears in search results about 470 times a day instead of 60–70, and the average position improved from 27 to 13. Clicks are now starting to follow (13 and 26 clicks on the last two days of the data).
  • Every page was audited and fixed for the basics Google checks: titles, descriptions, headings, links, images, structured data, and the robots file. The site now passes with zero issues on all 187 pages.
  • Broken and lost content was repaired: 15 broken links inside articles, two articles that had silently gone offline, one missing article redirected, and 26 new links from articles to the treks they describe.
  • Google titles and descriptions can now be edited for every page from the website's own admin, so the team is never dependent on a developer for SEO text.
  • Content programme started: a 6-month plan built on 3,346 real Google search phrases; the first new article (Mardi Himal Trek Difficulty) is written and awaiting approval; five half-finished drafts were researched, corrected and completed, ready to publish one at a time.

Where the site is already visible

  • Trek pages show the price directly in Google ("product snippets"): 53 clicks from 317 impressions, because of the product data we added to every trek page.
  • Foreign visitors are starting to see the site: United States 1,274 impressions, United Kingdom 495, India 492, Australia 281 in the period. Very few clicked yet: this is exactly what the new titles and the content programme are for.
  • Articles already on Google's page 1–2 with almost no clicks (Tilicho Lake 1,341 impressions; Nepal geography 548; How cold is Everest 384; Michael Matthews 375): these seven were given new titles and descriptions on 28 October. Results will be checked around 11 November.
Please note: most clicks recorded before 7 October came from the company's own devices (for example 32 clicks on the privacy policy page from position 87). These were excluded from the analysis. Google can detect and discount this kind of activity, so please do not search for and click the website from the office; it also makes the real results impossible to read.
